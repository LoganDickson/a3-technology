# 📌 Overview

The Technology Department proposes converting aging Windows laptops into ChromeOS Flex devices. This initiative aligns with our district’s Google Workspace environment, extends the useful life of existing hardware, and reduces costs and maintenance overhead.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To improve performance, security, and manageability of outdated laptops by repurposing them with ChromeOS Flex—Google’s lightweight, secure, cloud-based operating system.

---

## ✅ Key Benefits

|Area|Benefit|
|---|---|
|**Performance Boost**|Faster boot times and smoother performance on legacy hardware|
|**Security**|Built-in protections: verified boot, sandboxing, automatic updates|
|**Central Management**|Seamless integration with Google Admin Console|
|**Sustainability**|Reduces e-waste by repurposing older devices|
|**User Experience**|Simplifies access to Google tools and web-based resources|

---

## 📦 What Is ChromeOS Flex?

ChromeOS Flex is a free, cloud-first operating system from Google designed to modernize older PCs and Macs. It offers a near-Chromebook experience, optimized for speed, simplicity, and security—perfect for schools using Google Workspace.

---

## 🔄 Proposed Rollout Plan

|Phase|Timeline|Action|
|---|---|---|
|**Proposal**|Week 1|Present plan to district leadership|
|**Pilot Program**|Weeks 2–4|Convert 5–10 test devices across a few schools|
|**Feedback Review**|Week 5|Evaluate performance and staff/user feedback|
|**Planning**|Week 6|Finalize plan for broader rollout|
|**District Rollout**|Weeks 7–12|Begin full-scale device conversion and training as needed|

---

## 🧪 Pilot Goals

- Demonstrate improved device performance
    
- Test Google Admin Console management
    
- Evaluate staff and student experience
    
- Identify compatibility issues, if any
    

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

- Faster boot/login times
    
- Reduction in IT support tickets
    
- Staff satisfaction with usability
    
- Cost savings vs. device replacement
    
- Admin management effectiveness
    

---

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

|Risk|Mitigation|
|---|---|
|Incompatible software|Retain limited Windows devices for specialty use cases|
|Hardware not supported|Target known-compatible models first|
|Staff hesitation or confusion|Provide clear communication and support materials|
